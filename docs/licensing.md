# Licensing decision

Draft selected to match the requested policy: developers can use Linkoi in non-competing commercial applications, but cannot provide competing products or services under the standard license.

The repository contains the unmodified PolyForm Shield 1.0.0 text plus a copyright notice. Its competition clause is broader than hosted API resale: it also covers competing libraries and products, including free ones, and products the licensor or affiliates provide using the software. The New Products provision protects certain pre-existing uses. Read the exact terms before releasing, particularly because the author also operates Tabstash.

This is source-available software, not OSI-approved open source. No claim of legal clearance is made. Packages remain private to prevent accidental publication while the license and initial release are reviewed.
