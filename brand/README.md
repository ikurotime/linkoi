# Linkoi identity

The koi silhouette is the primary mark. Use the editable SVG, keeping its proportions and leaving at least a fin's width of clear space. Avoid adding scales, gradients, shadows, or Japanese decorative motifs.

- Koi orange: `#eb4034`. For the mark and decorative accents.
- Pond navy: `#1e264a`. Primary background.
- Paper: `#fff7ed`. Main text on navy.
- Mist: `#c6cbdf`. Supporting text on navy.

Use navy buttons with paper text. Keep orange for large marks and accents; it is not the body text color. The documentation uses the platform sans-serif and monospace stacks so it works without external font requests. The wordmark is lowercase **linkoi**; prose uses **Linkoi**.

`koi.svg` is transparent, `avatar.svg` includes the navy tile, and `banner.svg` is the repository banner. The source is licensed under PolyForm Shield 1.0.0. The license does not grant trademark rights; do not imply endorsement when using the Linkoi name or mark.

## Pixel koi

The symbol is drawn on a 24 × 24 grid, viewed from above, with a curved, tapered body, paired pectoral fins, a forked tail, and navy eyes. Keep its orange fill and square edges. Prefer multiples of 24 pixels for crisp display. The standalone SVG has a transparent background; the avatar uses navy. Avoid rotation, smoothing, or adding shading.

## Landing typography

Gambetta Medium is the hero display face. General Sans is used for navigation, headings, and body text. Both are served through Fontshare. IBM Plex Mono remains for code. Keep the display serif confined to the hero, and retain the clean sans-serif wordmark.
